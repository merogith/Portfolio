Master’s thesis · University of Europe for Applied Sciences · 2024

Revolutionizing Pharmaceutical Marketing with Generative AI

I surveyed 55 pharmaceutical professionals using the Technology Acceptance Model and compared perceived usefulness and ease of use across marketing tasks.

Perceived usefulness was positively associated with intention to use generative AI. The cross-sectional survey measured perceptions and intended use at one point in time.

The online survey used purposive recruitment through pharmaceutical managers and colleagues. It contained 47 questions and remained open for two weeks; the findings describe this sample rather than all pharmaceutical professionals.

ENVE 404 individual term project · Middle East Technical University · 2020

Calibrating a contaminant transport model

I simulated concentration over time and distance in MATLAB. At 60 m, I fitted the dispersion parameter to four observations by minimizing root mean square error and checked the fit against the three remaining observations.

RMSE was 0.37 g/m³ on the calibration points and 0.16 g/m³ on the held-out points. The check used one distance and three held-out values.

Source: ENVE 404 Term Project 1, 13 November 2020, Part B. The original report confirms the four/three observation split and best raw dispersion value of 1085. The assignment mixes year and day labels; this reproduces its numeric fit, not a physically validated field model.

Environmental engineering team design · Middle East Technical University · 2020

Sludgendary · Industrial wastewater design

Our team evaluated wastewater treatment and reuse options for an anonymized industrial zone with 144 facilities. The recommended design combined on-site corn-starch wastewater treatment with textile water reclamation.

Recommended treatment design

The team compared eight concepts and designed three alternatives. The selected scenario combined UASB, membrane bioreactor and reverse osmosis for corn-starch wastewater with textile water reclamation.

Slide 40 reports modeled present-value costs of $3,242,297.8 and 34,783,770 m³ treated over 20 years, giving the reported $0.093/m³. These are the 2020 coursework model’s assumptions and outputs, not a current construction quote or observed operating cost.

Internal planning workflow · EDGE Berlin · 2026

Event planning automation

I built a swipe-card interface for collecting input from event leads. Replies were stored in Supabase and forwarded through a Google Apps Script webhook to a shared Google Sheet. I embedded the interface in the team’s Notion planning space.

My 3 November 2026 implementation log records role-filtered question cards, saved test responses, and a live check that new answers appeared automatically in the sheet. The response data was organized into Answers and Questions tabs so card IDs could be matched to the relevant event, prompt and owner.

This is a case study of internal work. The production workspace and team responses are not a public demo. The illustrated card below contains invented content.

How the response path worked
  1. Event leads selected their role to see relevant planning cards.
  2. The interface stored a submitted response in Supabase.
  3. A database webhook sent new responses to a Google Apps Script receiver.
  4. The receiver added the response to a shared Google Sheet; the interface was embedded in Notion.

The work log records successful test submissions, not a reliability benchmark. It also notes follow-up checks for owner mapping, historic-response backfill and stronger access control before broader team use.

Project methodology · 2026

Regional Risk Index comparison

The project’s comparison method matches reporting units to the INFORM Risk Index and tests how similarly the two indices rank the same units. Spearman rank correlation summarizes agreement across the ranking. Checking overlap at the top shows whether the two methods would prioritize the same places.

The comparison measures agreement with a benchmark. Predictive validation would require testing against independently observed outcomes.
