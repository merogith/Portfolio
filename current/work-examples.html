I prepared CRM reporting data in SQL and worked with IT and local teams to trace duplicates and unmatched records. This fictional batch shows how to reconcile rows and surface exceptions before releasing migrated data to reporting.

Example release decision

Hold this batch from reporting: 12 staged rows have deliberately wrong target-ID mappings and inconsistent consent status. Reconciliation alone still balances, so it cannot detect this defect. Correct the mapped fields from the verified source records and rerun the same release gate.

The runnable example reports BLOCK before correction, then PASS for staging after an in-memory demo correction. All 220 held rows remain excluded.

Cash runway sensitivity

In this past role, I built recurring revenue and runway models in Power BI and prepared investor materials. This synthetic sample isolates how changes in cash inflows and outflows affect runway; it does not reproduce the employer’s finances.

Synthetic scenarios · TRY 1.2m available cash
ScenarioCash inCash outNet burnRunway
Downside180k380k200k6 mo
Base230k380k150k8 mo
Upside260k380k120k10 mo

Cash flows are monthly and in TRY. Runway divides starting cash by monthly cash outflows minus inflows, assuming flat cash flows and no new funding, financing costs or one-off costs. Negative projected balances represent a funding gap, not spendable cash.
